The Gym Aesthetic Trap

A data story about body standards, PEDs, Reddit angst, and the rise of performance over wellness.

📘 Overview

This project is a three-part data storytelling journey exploring modern gym culture, rising aesthetic expectations, the darker side of fitness influencers, and the narratives shared by real people online.
