The Gym Aesthetic Trap

A data story about body standards, PEDs, Reddit angst, and the rise of performance over wellness.

📘 What This Project Is

This is a three-part data storytelling project that explores how modern fitness has evolved into a performance and appearance-driven culture. Using real-world datasets, natural language processing, and visualization, we uncover how:

  • Gym costs have risen beyond median incomes
  • People Google "six-pack" and "steroids" more than "cardio" or "VO2 max"
  • Influencers are dying young — often linked to enhancement drugs
  • Reddit users talk more about aesthetics, anxiety, and "cycles" than strength or health

🎯 Final Takeaway
