The Gym Aesthetic Trap

A data story about how modern fitness shifted from health to hyper-performance — and what it’s costing us.

📘 Project Overview

This is a three-part interactive portfolio built with Python, NLP, and public datasets — exploring how aesthetics, social pressure, and enhancement culture are reshaping fitness.

📊 Chapter 1: Gym Inflation vs Income

Title: Is Fitness Becoming a Luxury?

This analysis investigates how gym membership costs in the U.S. evolved from 2000 to 2024 relative to median income. Results show gym prices have outpaced income growth — suggesting fitness is becoming increasingly inaccessible for the average person.

📈 View Chapter 1

🌐 Chapter 2: Google Trends & Influencer Health

Title: From Trends to Tragedy

Google Trends analysis from 2004–2024 shows how interest in aesthetics-based goals has eclipsed health-focused terms. This shift aligns with increasing visibility of enhancement shortcuts and a disturbing pattern of early deaths among fitness influencers.

🔍 View Chapter 2

💬 Chapter 3: Reddit NLP & Community Sentiment

Title: What Are Gym-Goers Really Talking About?

This final chapter uses sentiment analysis and topic modeling on 1000+ Reddit posts to surface real conversations in the fitness world. Key themes include anxiety around appearance, conflicting advice, and confusion about enhancement — revealing a culture of pressure and performance.
